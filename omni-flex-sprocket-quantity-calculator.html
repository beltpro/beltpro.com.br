Engineering Notes

  • The recommended quantity is rounded upward to the next whole sprocket.
  • Verify the final sprocket arrangement, shaft capacity, keyways, hubs and belt engagement before installation.
  • This calculator provides a preliminary engineering estimate and does not replace the belt manufacturer's installation requirements.

Sprocket Positioning

The sprockets must be positioned according to the belt openings, as shown below.

  • Drive shaft: place the first sprocket at the third opening from each belt edge. Position the remaining drive sprockets at equally spaced odd-numbered openings.
  • Idler shaft: place the first sprocket at the second opening from each belt edge. Position the remaining idler sprockets at equally spaced even-numbered openings.
  • Maintain symmetrical spacing across the belt width whenever possible.
