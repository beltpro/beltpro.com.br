Important engineering notes

  • This calculator is restricted to ANSI simplex roller chains.
  • Chain selection must use one consistent manufacturer's allowable working-load data, not minimum tensile strength.
  • The driven sprocket input must be its pitch diameter.
  • Low temperatures, corrosion, lubrication, alignment, starts and shock loading may require additional manufacturer derating.
  • The result is preliminary and must be verified against the selected chain manufacturer's current catalogue.
